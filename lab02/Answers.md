# Reflection and Analysis

## 1. Characteristics of phishing pages
A phishing page often has a wrong or suspicious URL, imitates a real login page, and tries to pressure you into entering credentials. In your materials, phishing is described as a fraudulent message or attack used to trick users, and users are told to watch for suspicious links.

## 2. How to protect yourself
Check the URL carefully, do not click suspicious links, use secure communication channels, and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA). User awareness and phishing training are specifically recommended in the course materials.

## 3. Why modern sites make this harder
Modern sites often use stronger authentication, especially MFA, and better security controls for digital services. That means stealing just a password from a fake page is often not enough anymore.
